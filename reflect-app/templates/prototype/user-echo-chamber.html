Important Nuance

Echo chambers aren't inherently bad. Sometimes consistency creates comfort, community, or shared understanding. The concern is when repetition limits your exposure to new ideas or alternative perspectives.

Low repetition doesn't guarantee diverse thinking—it just means you're seeing content from different sources. Quality and depth of perspective matter too.
