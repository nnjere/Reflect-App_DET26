Variety vs. Narrow Focus

High diversity

Exposure to many topics, perspectives, and formats. Can feel stimulating but sometimes scattered.

Low diversity

Focus on specific topics or niches. Can feel cohesive but might limit exposure to new ideas.

Important Nuance

High diversity doesn't automatically mean better. Too much variety can feel overwhelming or prevent you from going deep on topics you care about. Low diversity isn't inherently limiting—it might reflect focused interests.

The goal is awareness: Does this level of diversity feel right for what you want from your feed? Are you learning new things, or just skimming the surface?
